Brief Bio

I received my PhD from the School of Computer Science at Carnegie Mellon University working under Prof. William W. Cohen. I have worked as a scientist and/or engineering manager in institutions such as Microsoft, Snapchat, Qualcomm, Inome and Ericsson.

I have multiple patents, conference papers, and 20+ years of R&D experience in Data Science, Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Natural Language Processing, Search, Analytics, Data Mining, Information Retrieval and Information Extraction. More details can be found in my Google Scholar page.
